When Your Height Is Outside the Standard Range

All scenarios · 繁體中文

Short answer. A very tall or very short person is often sold a standard suit with longer or shorter sleeves and legs. The proportions of the jacket, the waist position and the button position are different on a different frame. AETERNAL drafts them from your own measurements.

Who this page is for

Men and women who are much taller or shorter than the range that standard sizes are built for. For very petite women, see also the page on petite proportions linked below.

The Axiom is AETERNAL's women's suit and The Singularity is its men's suit.

What often goes wrong with standard sizing

Length is not the only difference

Making sleeves and trousers longer or shorter does not change where the waist, the pockets or the buttons fall relative to the body.

A jacket that overwhelms or ends too soon

On a short frame a jacket cut for a taller person can look large and long. On a tall frame a standard jacket can end above where it should.

Reach and sitting

A tall person's arms reach further and the seated position is different. A short person's jacket can bunch at the hip when sitting.

Alteration limits

A tailor can change lengths and some widths. Moving the position of the shoulder, waist or buttons is a pattern change, which alteration cannot do.

How AETERNAL approaches it

  • Jacket length, waist position, pocket position and button position are drafted from your own measurements.
  • The Calibration Garment is worn so proportions and movement can be checked before the final cloth is cut.
  • Your feedback is recorded through the AOI, covering where the garment pulls, presses or restricts.
