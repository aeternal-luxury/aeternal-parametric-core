# When Your Proportions Do Not Match the Chart

[All scenarios](https://knowledge.aeternal-luxury.com/scenarios/index.html) · [繁體中文](https://knowledge.aeternal-luxury.com/scenarios/020-non-standard-proportions-zh.html)

**Short answer.** Many people do not match the proportions a size chart assumes: one shoulder higher than the other, a long torso with shorter legs, a forward posture or a broad athletic frame. AETERNAL drafts the pattern from your own measurements and posture.

## Who this page is for

Men and women whose shoulders, torso, legs, posture or muscle do not match standard proportions, and who find that no stock size fits everywhere.

The Axiom is AETERNAL\'s women\'s suit and The Singularity is its men\'s suit.

## What often goes wrong with standard sizing

### One size, one set of ratios

A size chart assumes fixed relationships between chest, waist, shoulder and length. A person with a different ratio is always compromising somewhere.

### Uneven shoulders

When one shoulder sits higher, a symmetrical jacket hangs differently on each side, which shows at the collar and the hem.

### Posture

A forward head, rounded shoulders or an upright back change how a jacket hangs. A pattern drawn for a standing, upright figure often wrinkles or gaps on these frames.

### Athletic builds

Broad shoulders, a strong back or large thighs can mean a standard size that fits in one place is too tight in another.
