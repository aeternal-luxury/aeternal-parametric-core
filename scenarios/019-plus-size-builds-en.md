# A Suit Made for the Body You Have

[All scenarios](https://knowledge.aeternal-luxury.com/scenarios/index.html) · [繁體中文](https://knowledge.aeternal-luxury.com/scenarios/019-plus-size-builds-zh.html)

**Short answer.** Plus-size suits are often a smaller pattern made wider. Bodies do not widen evenly. AETERNAL drafts the pattern from your own measurements and posture, and checks it on a physical Calibration Garment standing and seated.

## Who this page is for

Men and women with larger builds who want a suit made for their own proportions rather than a standard size scaled up.

The Axiom is AETERNAL\'s women\'s suit and The Singularity is its men\'s suit.

## What often goes wrong with standard sizing

### Widening is not drafting

Standard grading adds width in even steps. Volume sits differently on different bodies, for example at the chest, the abdomen, the hips or the upper arm.

### Pulling at the button

A jacket that is too tight across the middle pulls at the button and creates creases that point at the strain.

### Sleeves and armholes

A sleeve or armhole that is tight on the upper arm limits movement and shows it when the arm moves.

### Sitting

A suit that fits standing can ride up, pull or bunch when seated, which matters at long meals, ceremonies and meetings.

## How AETERNAL approaches it

- The pattern starts from your own measurements and posture, standing and seated.
- The Calibration Garment is worn so pressure points and movement can be checked before the final cloth is cut.
- Your feedback is recorded through the AOI, covering where the garment presses, pulls or restricts.
- The final suit is hand-finished with full-canvas construction.
