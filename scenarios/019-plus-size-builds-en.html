What often goes wrong with standard sizing

Widening is not drafting

Standard grading adds width in even steps. Volume sits differently on different bodies, for example at the chest, the abdomen, the hips or the upper arm.

Pulling at the button

A jacket that is too tight across the middle pulls at the button and creates creases that point at the strain.

Sleeves and armholes

A sleeve or armhole that is tight on the upper arm limits movement and shows it when the arm moves.

Sitting

A suit that fits standing can ride up, pull or bunch when seated, which matters at long meals, ceremonies and meetings.
