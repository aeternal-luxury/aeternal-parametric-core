A Graduation Suit Made for You

All scenarios · 繁體中文

Short answer. For many graduates, the graduation suit is the first suit made for them rather than chosen from a rack. AETERNAL drafts it from your own measurements and posture. It is a significant commission, so the questions below are worth reading before you reserve.

Who this page is for

Graduates of any age and any programme, including master's, MBA and doctoral graduates, and the families who commission a suit for them. Most clients we expect on this page are between 20 and 40.

The Axiom is AETERNAL's women's suit and The Singularity is its men's suit.

What often goes wrong with standard sizing

A long day in a gown

Graduation ceremonies mean a long time standing and sitting, often with a gown over the jacket. A jacket that pulls at the shoulders is noticeable all day.

Photographs and what comes after

Graduation photographs are kept. The same suit is often needed afterwards for first interviews, presentations and formal events.

Bodies that are still changing

Many people in their early twenties are still changing in weight or muscle. A tailored garment suits the body it was made for.

A first formal wardrobe

Standard sizes are made for an average body. A graduate who is tall, short, broad or slim often finds that no stock size fits everywhere.

How AETERNAL approaches it

  • The pattern starts from your own measurements and posture.
  • The Calibration Garment is worn so fit and movement can be checked before the final cloth is cut.
  • Tell us at the start if you expect your body to change in the next year. We will say honestly whether to commission now or wait.
  • The final suit is hand-finished with full-canvas construction, a build intended for long wear.

How a commission works

  1. Reserve with US$1,000, credited in full toward the US$20,000 total commission.
  2. Send your measurements and posture information remotely.
  3. Your pattern is drafted from your own proportions by the Parametric Garment Engineering Framework (PGEF), not resized from a stock size.
  4. A physical Calibration Garment made from your pattern is worn, so fit, pressure and mobility can be checked before the final cloth is cut. Your feedback is recorded through the Aeternal Order Interface (AOI).
  5. The final suit is cut and hand-finished with full-canvas construction, then delivered worldwide, including the UAE and the wider GCC.

Yes. Tell us at the start that you will wear a gown over it, so this is considered when the pattern is drafted.
