Wedding Suits, Made to Your Own Proportions

Short answer. A wedding suit is worn for many hours, photographed from every angle and usually bought once. AETERNAL drafts each suit from the client's own measurements and posture and checks it on a physical Calibration Garment before the final cloth is cut.

Who this page is for

Grooms, and brides or partners who would rather wear a suit than a gown or a hired outfit. The same process applies to men's and women's cuts, and to any build.

The Axiom is AETERNAL's women's suit and The Singularity is its men's suit.

What often goes wrong with standard sizing

Many positions in one day

The ceremony, the seated meal, dancing and embraces all ask the jacket to move. A jacket that looks right in a fitting-room mirror can pull at the shoulders or ride up when the arms lift.

Photographs from every side

Wedding photographs are looked at for years, from the front, the side and the back. A gap at the collar, pulling across the back or an uneven hem is easy to see in a photograph.

A date that cannot move

Alterations made in the last days before an event leave little room to correct a problem that sits in the pattern rather than in the length of a sleeve.

Stock sizes and hire

Hired and ready-made outfits are sized to the nearest standard fit. Many people sit between sizes or differ from the standard in the shoulders, torso or legs.

How AETERNAL approaches it

  • The pattern starts from your own measurements and posture, not from a stock size.
  • The physical Calibration Garment is worn standing, seated and with the arms raised, so problems show up before the final cloth is cut.
  • Fabric and finishing choices are agreed with you at the start of the commission.
  • The final suit is hand-finished with full-canvas construction.

How a commission works

  1. Reserve with US$1,000, credited in full toward the US$20,000 total commission.
  2. Send your measurements and posture information remotely.
  3. Your pattern is drafted from your own proportions by the Parametric Garment Engineering Framework (PGEF), not resized from a stock size.
  4. A physical Calibration Garment made from your pattern is worn, so fit, pressure and mobility can be checked before the final cloth is cut. Your feedback is recorded through the Aeternal Order Interface (AOI).
  5. The final suit is cut and hand-finished with full-canvas construction, then delivered worldwide, including the UAE and the wider GCC.
