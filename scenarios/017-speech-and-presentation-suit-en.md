# A Suit for the Stage, the Podium and the Camera

[All scenarios](https://knowledge.aeternal-luxury.com/scenarios/index.html) · [繁體中文](https://knowledge.aeternal-luxury.com/scenarios/017-speech-and-presentation-suit-zh.html)

**Short answer.** Speakers move, gesture and sit on stage, and are often recorded. A suit for this work has to stay in order when the arms lift and when the speaker sits. AETERNAL drafts it from your own proportions and checks it on a physical Calibration Garment.

## Who this page is for

Speakers, keynote presenters, panellists, awardees, founders who pitch, and anyone who is recorded or watched at a distance while they speak. Men\'s and women\'s cuts, any build.

The Axiom is AETERNAL\'s women\'s suit and The Singularity is its men\'s suit.

## What often goes wrong with standard sizing

### Arms that lift

Gesturing, pointing and reaching for a microphone raise the arms. A jacket with a tight shoulder or armhole lifts at the collar and shows it.

### Standing, then seated

Panels and ceremonies ask for both. A jacket that suits a standing figure can bunch or pull when the speaker sits.

### Microphone packs and lighting

A body-worn microphone pack and stage lighting both show up the jacket\'s shape. The garment has to carry them without distorting.

### Cameras and screens

On a recording or a screen, creases, pulling and collar gaps are enlarged. Details that go unnoticed in a room are visible on video.

## How AETERNAL approaches it

- The pattern starts from your own measurements, posture and the way you move when you speak.
- The Calibration Garment is worn standing, seated and with the arms raised, and your feedback is recorded through the AOI.
- Tell us at the start about microphone packs, a podium or a stage chair, and these are considered in the design.
- The final suit is hand-finished with full-canvas construction.
