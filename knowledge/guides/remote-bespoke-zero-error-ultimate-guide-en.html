1. The Time-Space Prison of Traditional Bespoke: Trunk Shows and Waiting

Traditional Bespoke requires you to visit a specific city's atelier and complete 3-4 fittings over several months. For globally traveling executives, this is a form of punishment. Even prestige "Trunk Shows" simply air-freight tailors to a hotel to occupy your weekend — the environment is rushed and error rates are high. These models are inherently inefficient because they depend on physical-world synchronization — you must conform to the tailor's schedule.

2. Return on Time Capital (ROTC): Why Waiting is a Loss

Each fitting consumes 3-5 hours (including travel, waiting, adjustment). Traditional bespoke requires at least 3 fittings, totaling 15 hours. For decision-makers billing thousands per hour, this represents tens of thousands of dollars in hidden cost. More importantly, that time should have been allocated to decisions, transactions, or strategic thinking.

Traditional bespoke total time = 15 hours × Hourly Rate = $75,000 (assuming $5,000/hour)
AETERNAL remote calibration total time = 1 hour (initial) + 30 min (feedback) = 1.5 hours

4. Phase 01: Empirical Calibration Chassis — Physical Data Probe

This is not a standard sample garment. It's a semi-finished product generated from your initial data, specifically designed to collect dynamic data. Its fabric and structure allow precise recording of tension data in specific zones. You simply wear it during daily activities (sitting, standing, reaching, turning) for 2-3 days, then record your experience.

5. Phase 02: AOI Interface — Your Feedback is Data

The AOI (Active Override Interface) is a secure online portal where you can mark specific locations on the chassis (e.g., "right shoulder back is too tight," "left armpit feels pulled"). The system translates these subjective descriptions into numerical deviations and feeds them into the Q-Matrix for automatic correction. Every piece of your feedback is a precise micro-adjustment of the final geometric shell.

6. Global Delivery: Encrypted Production & International Logistics

The corrected production files are AE-ID hashed and encrypted, then sent to our production node. Files contain no identifiable biometric information — only geometric instructions. After production, they ship via DHL/FedEx priority logistics to any address globally. Typically 7-10 working days from calibration completion to delivery.
