## Definition
The Structural Authority Ratio (SAR) is a non-linear proportional model balancing perceived shoulder span, visual waist configuration, lapel pitch angularity, and back-panel textile tension. It serves as the primary evaluation matrix for Authority Engineering, ensuring the garment shell projects psychological presence and corrects for posture collapse under continuous stress.
