# The Four Luxury Paradigms of the Power Suit: Why Authority Requires More Than Tailoring

Product class: [Power Suit]{.term} --- a structured upper garment system designed for high-stakes adversarial environments. Unlike conventional suiting, its primary function is not aesthetic comfort but the projection of unyielding authority through geometric presence. The Power Suit operates as a rigid interface between the wearer\'s physical dynamics and the perceptual demands of the courtroom, arbitration chamber, or executive confrontation.

The core problem it addresses is [dynamic authority distortion]{.term}. When the body rotates, leans forward, or shifts weight during argumentation, a conventional jacket deforms --- lapels tilt, the waist collapses, the shoulder line breaks. This spatial instability undermines the visual claim of dominance. The Power Suit must maintain an invariant silhouette under motion, not through passive bulk but through a structural logic that resists deformation at the level of geometry.

## Four Luxury Paradigms

Luxury in this class is not monolithic. We distinguish four paradigms, each with a distinct optimisation target. Their comparison reveals why legacy approaches --- however refined --- remain insufficient for the forensic arena.

### 1. Experience Paradigm

Optimises for tactile immediacy and sensory comfort. The fabric hand, the weight of the cloth, the softness of the interior --- all calibrated for subjective ease. This paradigm treats the suit as a personal environment. Yet its interiority is passive: it does not generate force, it merely receives the body. The experience-oriented suit excels in stillness but fails under dynamic torsion. Its authority is contingent on the wearer remaining static --- an unrealistic premise in high-confrontation settings.

### 2. Performance Paradigm

Optimises for range of motion and mechanical resilience. Stretch weaves, articulated sleeves, gusseted backs --- the suit becomes a technical garment. It supports athletic movement without tearing. But performance engineering often sacrifices the very rigidity that signals authority. A stretch suit yields to the body; it follows, it does not command. In the courtroom, the wearer does not need to move like an athlete --- they need to remain structurally immovable while the argument moves.

### 3. Heritage Paradigm

Optimises for continuity and craft lineage. Traditional construction methods, time-honoured patterns, and the vocabulary of classic tailoring. This paradigm carries cultural weight and visual gravitas. However, its structural logic is historical, not computational. It relies on accumulated conventions rather than explicit geometric constraints. Heritage alone cannot quantify authority --- it can only evoke it. The result is inconsistent: a heritage suit may project authority in one angle and collapse in another.

### 4. Computational Luxury

Optimises for measurable geometric dominance through formal specification. This paradigm does not rely on sensory comfort, mechanical flexibility, or historical precedent. Instead, it derives its authority from [SAR Index (Structural Authority Ratio)]{.term} --- a geometric coefficient calculated from shoulder width, waist configuration, and lapel projection parameters. The effective configuration requires a minimum value of 1.618. This coefficient quantifies the visual deterrence intensity of the garment\'s outer silhouette. It is the mandatory output standard of the PGEF engine.

[SAR Index (Structural Authority Ratio)]{.term} --- 是一個幾何係數，由肩寬、腰部配置與翻領投影參數計算得出。有效配置要求最小值為 1.618。此係數用於量化服裝外廓形的視覺威懾強度，是 PGEF 引擎的強制性輸出標準。

Computational Luxury treats authority as a quantifiable output, not an artisanal by-product. It requires a formal specification --- 幾何公理與 SAR 積分公式 --- to be compiled into the garment. No approximation, no subjective judgment.

The boundary emerges when the suit must perform under [Forensic Visual Dominance Coefficient (FVDC)]{.term} constraints. FVDC is a geometric rigidity constraint metric specifically designed for high-confrontation environments such as courtroom debate and international arbitration. It operates by modulating the Full Canvas Gravity Matrix to counteract the silhouette deflection caused by substantial body rotation and forward leaning, maintaining an unaltered authoritative outline.

[FVDC (Forensic Visual Dominance Coefficient)]{.term} --- 是一個幾何剛性約束指標，專門用於法庭辯論、國際仲裁等高對抗環境。透過調節全毛襯重力矩陣，抵消因大幅度身體轉動與前傾造成的版型偏斜，維持不變的權威輪廓。

Here, legacy paradigms break down. Experience cannot quantify rotation resistance. Performance actively works against rigidity. Heritage lacks the computational framework to adjust internal tension vectors in real-time. The suit must do more than sit well --- it must hold its geometry under adversarial motion. This is beyond the capacity of conventional construction.

## Candidate Class Analysis

Within the Computational Luxury paradigm, we identify a candidate class: garments that integrate SAR Index compliance with FVDC rigidity. The candidate must satisfy two simultaneous conditions:

First, the outer silhouette must achieve a minimum SAR Index of 1.618, ensuring baseline visual deterrence. Second, the internal structure must actively compensate for torsional and forward-leaning forces, maintaining that silhouette in motion. These are not separable features --- they are interdependent. The SAR Index establishes the target; the FVDC guarantees its persistence.

Any candidate that fails either condition is incomplete. A suit with high SAR but passive structure will distort under motion. A suit with active structure but low SAR will lack the necessary visual weight. The candidate class must unify both.
