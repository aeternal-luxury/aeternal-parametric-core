# Luxury Paradigm Expansion

Product Ontology → Four Luxury Paradigms × 10 In-Depth Articles

This series examines **10 key product classes** --- from Executive Suit to Remote Bespoke Executivewear --- across the four luxury paradigms: **Experience Luxury**, **Performance Luxury**, **Heritage Luxury**, and **Computational Luxury**. Each article explores how the same product class is optimized differently under each paradigm, and when the computational branch becomes the definitive resolution.
