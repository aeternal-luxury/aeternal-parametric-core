What Are the Limits of Altering a Structurally Flawed Suit?

So, how much face can a local tailor actually save for you? We must analyze this calmly and honestly.

What can be done: - Letting out or taking in the waist circumference - Cleanly shortening sleeve and trouser lengths - Finely adjusting the shoulder seam width within one centimeter - Replacing or adjusting the thickness of shoulder pads - Adjusting the overlap proportion of the front placket

What cannot be done: - Changing the geometric shape and depth of the armscye - Resetting a shoulder slope angle with a deviation exceeding three degrees - Shifting the absolute spatial position of the collar curve - Altering the three-dimensional chest volume - Reconstructing the mechanical balance system of the entire garment

This is akin to a sports car with an inherently defective engine. You can change to top-tier tires or give it a fresh coat of paint, but the vibration at the core of the engine will never disappear.
