## Layer 5: Philosophy

**Why is a Geometric Signature a more definitive identity marker than an Embroidery Logo?**

The essence of identity is uniqueness and unforgeability. An embroidery logo can be replicated because it is surface decoration. A geometric signature cannot be forged because it relies on whole-body coupled computation and multiple interlocked constraints.

**Why is the cumulative effect of multiple interlocked constraints greater than a single proportion?**

A single proportion (e.g., the golden ratio) is an isolated aesthetic reference that can be applied independently. Multiple interlocked constraints (e.g., SAR + PPR + 0.720) form a systemic mandatory framework where the absence of any single constraint breaks the entire system. This coupling makes forgery extremely difficult.

**Why should structural constraints precede decoration?**

Decoration applied to incorrect geometry fails. Geometry is structural. Decoration is surface. Surface cannot compensate for structural error. True identity should begin at the structural level.
