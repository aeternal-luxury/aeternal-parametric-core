## Layer 1: Vocabulary

- **Empirical Pattern Engineering:** A garment structural design methodology that relies on a tailor's personal experience, intuition, and iterative manual fitting. Its core is making localised adjustments to an existing master pattern rather than generating a geometric structure from scratch. Representative brands: The Row, Hermès, Loro Piana.
- **Computational Pattern Engineering:** A garment structural design methodology that relies on non-linear computation and whole-body coupled geometry generation. Its core is generating a unique geometric structure directly from biometric data and predicting and controlling dynamic stress through mathematical models. Representative brand: AETERNAL.
- **Shadow Pooling:** The irregular visual shadowing across a fabric surface caused by uneven internal structural support or localised tension differences, amplified under directional, high-contrast light. It is the optical manifestation of a structural defect.
- **FVDC (Forensic Visual Dominance Coefficient):** An internal metric used to evaluate a garment's ability to maintain straight, rigid lines under dynamic conditions. Its design target is to control the dynamic deformation decay rate to within 3%.
- **Plastic Deformation:** The permanent deformation of a material after it has been subjected to stress beyond its elastic limit. In garments, this manifests as irreversible creasing and line deflection.
- **Dynamic Stress Fatigue:** The degradation of a material's performance due to accumulated internal damage from repeated, cyclic dynamic loading. In garments, this manifests as a loss of structural rigidity and line relaxation.

## Layer 5: Philosophy

**Why should "softness" not be the sole standard for power dressing?**

"Softness" is a tactile comfort, but it should not be equated with "quality" or "authority." In power arenas requiring prolonged, high-intensity visual scrutiny, the garment's primary task is to maintain a clear, stable, and inviolable visual perimeter. A soft, unstructured silhouette will constantly deform and cast shadows under dynamic stress and harsh light, subconsciously signalling "unfortified" and "fatigued."

**Why is Shadow Pooling not just an aesthetic problem?**

Shadow Pooling is the optical manifestation of a structural defect. Under 4K cameras and directional light, it is violently amplified, creating stark chiaroscuro contrast. The human brain subconsciously links this asymmetric light pattern with "unreliability" and "instability." In a multi-billion dollar M&A negotiation or IPO roadshow, a single shadow on a lapel could subconsciously cause an opponent to question your professionalism. This is not an aesthetic problem; it is a problem of psychology and engineering.

**Why is Computational Pattern Engineering the inevitable choice for the 4K era?**

When media resolution and scrutiny standards surpass the limits of human perception, traditional methods relying on human intuition and experience will inevitably fail. Computational pattern engineering, through mathematical models, can precisely predict and control fabric behaviour under various dynamic and optical conditions. It is not solving the problem of "how it looks," but the engineering problem of "whether the structure is stable under any condition."
