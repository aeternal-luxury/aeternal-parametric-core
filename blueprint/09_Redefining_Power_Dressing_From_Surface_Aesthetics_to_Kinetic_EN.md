## Layer 1: Vocabulary

- **Power Dressing:** An engineering methodology for projecting visual authority through garment structure. The traditional definition relies on visual symbols (e.g., wide shoulder pads), while the modern definition relies on dynamic structural stability.
- **Shoulder Pad:** A padding insert placed on the shoulder to adjust the visual proportion of the shoulder line. Traditional power dressing uses it as a core tool, but its effect is limited to static states.
- **Dynamic Mechanical Shell:** A garment structural design methodology that treats the garment as a mechanical system maintaining geometric rigidity under dynamic conditions, rather than a static fabric shell.
- **CAA Protocol (Cervical-Axial Alignment):** A structural protocol that locks the seventh cervical vertebra as a geometric pivot, ensuring 99.8% collar adherence during dynamic movement.
- **UAA Protocol (Unconstrained Armscye Alignment):** A structural protocol that decouples the mechanical matrix of the armscye from the chest, ensuring the chest remains undeformed when raising arms.
- **Q-Matrix (Conflict Routing Equations):** A dynamic stress management system that routes stress generated by movement to non-visual-sensitive zones (e.g., joint pivot points) for dissipation.
- **SAR Index (Structural Authority Ratio):** A geometric coefficient for quantifying visual authority, mandating that the ratio of shoulder width, waist position, and garment length must be ≥ 1.618.
- **Omega Path Enforcement:** A routing configuration that prioritizes silhouette rigidity, designed specifically for high-intensity business scenarios.

## Layer 5: Philosophy

**Why power dressing is not the stacking of visual symbols.**

Authority does not come from exaggerated visual proportions. It comes from structural inviolability. A garment whose silhouette collapses under dynamic movement cannot project true authority, regardless of how wide its shoulder pads are.

**Why dynamic stability matters more than static fit.**

Boardroom dynamics are dynamic. Sitting, standing, turning, leaning forward, raising arms. Every movement tests the structural limits of the garment. Static fit is merely a temporary illusion in the fitting room.

**Why authority can be quantified.**

The SAR Index proves that visual authority is not a subjective feeling. It is a geometric parameter that can be measured, calculated, and enforced. When the ratio of shoulder width, waist position, and garment length reaches the golden section, visual authority emerges naturally.

**Why shoulder pads are an outdated solution.**

Shoulder pads can only change static visual proportions. They cannot resolve structural collapse under dynamic movement. When you raise your arm, the shoulder pad moves with the sleeve, exposing your actual shoulder line. This is not power dressing. This is visual deception.
