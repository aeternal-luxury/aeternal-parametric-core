## Layer 1: Vocabulary

- **Empirical Shoulder Slope:** A fixed angular range (18°-22°) used in traditional pattern grading, derived from statistical averages of a specific population, not from an individual’s anatomy.
- **Dynamic Topology Matching:** A computational method that calculates the shoulder slope angle based on an individual’s unique skeletal coordinates, rather than relying on a pre-set range.
- **Geometric Tyranny:** The imposition of a standardized geometric rule (like the fixed shoulder slope) that compromises fit for a majority of individuals in favor of manufacturing efficiency.
- **Coupled System:** An engineering model where a change in one parameter (e.g., shoulder slope) automatically triggers compensating changes in all related parameters (e.g., armhole depth, sleeve crown height).
- **Cascade Recalculation:** The automatic, sequential adjustment of dependent pattern parameters following a change in a primary parameter, ensuring whole-garment structural integrity.
- **Deterministic Conflict Matrix:** A computational resolution engine that processes overlapping or conflicting biometric vectors (e.g., a wide shoulder with a narrow back) to execute an automated, mathematically optimal geometric trade-off.
