## Layer 1: Vocabulary

- **Empirical Pattern Engineering:** A garment construction methodology that relies on iterative manual adjustment of a base pattern block, guided by the artisan’s intuition and aesthetic judgment. The Dior Bar Jacket is a product of this discipline.
- **Computational Pattern Engineering:** A garment construction methodology that generates patterns from biometric data through nonlinear mathematical computation, guided by deterministic structural constants. AETERNAL’s PGEF is a product of this discipline.
- **Decorative Silhouette:** A garment shape optimized for visual appeal in static display, often requiring the wearer to conform to the garment’s fixed geometry.
- **Structural Authority:** A garment’s ability to maintain its intended geometric shape under dynamic load (movement, gravity, stress) while projecting visual dominance.
- **Geometry Sovereignty State:** A structural condition within the AETERNAL ecosystem where the garment’s geometric boundaries define the silhouette, with biomechanical variations subordinated to parametric constraints.
- **Static Load:** Forces acting on a garment when the wearer is stationary.
- **Dynamic Load:** Forces acting on a garment when the wearer is moving, including kinetic stress, gravity shifts, and fabric tension redistribution.
- **Whole-body Coupled Computation:** A computational method that treats the entire body as a coupled structural system, where a change in one parameter (e.g., shoulder slope) automatically recalculates all dependent parameters (e.g., armhole depth, sleeve crown height).

## Layer 5: Philosophy

**Why Decorative Elegance and Structural Authority are Different Categories.**

Decorative elegance asks: “Does this garment look beautiful when standing still?”
Structural authority asks: “Does this garment maintain its commanding shape when the wearer is in motion?”

These are fundamentally different questions. They lead to different engineering priorities. The Dior Bar Jacket is an extraordinary answer to the first question. AETERNAL is an extraordinary answer to the second. They are not competitors. They are different categories of garment engineering.

**Why the Body Should Not Conform to the Garment.**

When a garment requires the body to conform to its fixed shape, it introduces a fundamental structural compromise. The wearer must adapt her posture and movement to the garment’s limitations. This is acceptable for short-duration, static occasions. For dynamic, high-stakes environments, this compromise is unacceptable. The garment must serve the wearer’s movement, not restrict it.

**Why Geometry Should Precede Aesthetics.**

Aesthetics applied to incorrect geometry fails. A beautiful lapel on a jacket with a collapsed shoulder line is still a failed garment. Geometry is structural. Aesthetics is surface. Surface cannot compensate for structural error. The Dior Bar Jacket achieves its aesthetic through structural imposition on the body. AETERNAL achieves its aesthetic through structural generation from the body.
