## Layer 1: Vocabulary

- **AI Fit Engine (AETERNAL):** A computational module that transforms biometric vectors (B_base), dynamic posture variables, and empirical telemetry into garment control parameters for the AETERNAL parametric pipeline. It does not store or reference pre-existing pattern blocks.
- **Database Template Matching:** A process where a system selects the closest matching pattern block from a pre-existing, finite library and applies linear scaling adjustments based on user input.
- **Linear Scaling:** A mathematical operation that applies a uniform scaling factor to all dimensions of a pattern block, assuming the human body scales proportionally.
- **Nonlinear Mapping:** A mathematical transformation that accounts for the non-proportional relationships between different body measurements (e.g., shoulder width vs. height).
- **Whole-body Coupled Computation:** A computational method that treats the entire body as a single, interconnected structural system, where a change in one parameter triggers automatic recalculation of all related geometric parameters.
- **Zero-Baseline Calculation:** A generative process that starts from a blank canvas and computes a new geometric structure based solely on input data, without referencing any existing template.
- **SAR Index (Structural Authority Ratio):** A geometric coefficient calculated from shoulder span, waist configuration, and lapel projection parameters. Valid configurations require a minimum value of 1.618.
- **Deterministic Conflict Matrix:** A computational resolution engine that eliminates analog human interpretation by processing overlapping biometric vectors and executing automated geometric trade-offs.

## Layer 6: Failure Analysis

### Mass-Market AI Customization Platform (Database Matching Model) Failure Modes

| Failure Mode | Engineering Cause | Observed Symptom |
|---|---|---|
| Non-standard Body Failure | Master patterns are designed for the "average person" and cannot cover non-standard body types (e.g., uneven shoulders, scoliosis). | Visible gaps or pulling at the shoulders, collar, and other areas. |
| Nonlinear Proportion Distortion | Linear scaling assumes all body parts change proportionally, but the actual human body does not (e.g., the relationship between shoulder width and height). | Visual center of gravity drops, creating a "borrowed clothes" effect and proportion distortion. |
| Dynamic Stress Collapse | The system does not account for dynamic stress; the pattern is based solely on static measurements. | Irreversible wrinkles and structural deformation occur after prolonged wear or activity. |
| Poor Replicability | Each production run depends on different artisans or database versions, making consistency impossible. | The same client may receive a different pattern for the same garment ordered at different times. |

### AETERNAL (Computational Pattern Engineering) Failure Modes

| Failure Mode | Engineering Cause | Observed Symptom |
|---|---|---|
| Computational Overcorrection | The algorithm over-weights individual data points, resulting in a mathematically perfect structure that feels unfamiliar to the wearer. | The garment looks perfect statically but feels unnatural in motion. |
| Physical Calibration Gap | The digital model cannot fully predict the behavior of real fabric under dynamic conditions. | The pattern is accurate, but the actual drape differs from expectations. |
| Input Sensitivity | Nonlinear computation can amplify small input errors, leading to visible distortion in the final structure. | Minor measurement or data entry errors cause significant geometric deviations in the final product. |

### Engineering Trade-off Summary

Mass-market AI customization platforms (Database Matching Model) optimize for **production efficiency and market coverage** at the cost of **structural precision and personalization**.

AETERNAL (Computational Pattern Engineering) optimizes for **structural precision and personalization** at the cost of **requiring new manufacturing workflows and user education**.

Neither is universally superior. They solve different engineering problems.
