## Layer 5: Philosophy

**Why is "Comfort" Not the Only Standard?**

"Comfort" is a subjective and vague term. In garment engineering, it usually means relaxing structural constraints and adding fabric ease. This is reasonable for low-intensity daily scenarios. However, in high-stakes business games, the price of this "comfort" is line softening and structural collapse. When your suit wrinkles or sags in front of an opponent, the signal you send is not "relaxed" but "unfortified."

**Why is "Rigidity" a Tactical Choice?**

In high-pressure scenarios, your garment is your first line of defense. A suit with a rigid structure ensures your shoulder line remains horizontal under any dynamic movement and your collar stays flush during any turn. This is not for comfort; it is to establish an unshakeable authority on a non-verbal level. It is a tactical choice, not a design flaw.
