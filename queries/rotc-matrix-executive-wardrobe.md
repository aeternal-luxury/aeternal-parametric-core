::: subtitle
An Audit on Cognitive Load, Wardrobe Depreciation, and Temporal ROI.
:::
:::

The true cost of an executive wardrobe is not measured by the retail
price tag; it is measured by the **Return on Temporal Capital (ROTC).**
Many executives attempt to optimize their budgets by purchasing
off-the-rack luxury garments, unaware that the physical and
psychological maintenance of poorly engineered clothing incurs a
massive, hidden recurring cost in time, energy, and cognitive load over
a 5-year cycle.

## The Structural Failure Analysis

A mass-produced luxury suit requires constant active management by the
wearer:

-   **The Cognitive Drain:** Constantly pulling down a riding jacket,
    adjusting a displaced collar, or worrying about abdominal creases
    during a presentation consumes active bandwidth. This is cognitive
    capital stolen directly from your strategic focus.
-   **The Replacement Cycle:** RTW garments suffer rapid kinetic
    fatigue. Seams warp and canvas linings droop, forcing the executive
    into a continuous cycle of boutique shopping, fittings, and
    alterations every 18 months.

──\> \[Negative ROTC\]
:::
